# From sketch to slide

## Overview
- Audience: a technical presenter starting a new Vizz project.
- Question: how do we turn a rough idea into a clear, progressive explanation?
- Takeaway: agree on meaning and reveal order before polishing the animation.
- Format: two interactive slides, light theme, no LaTeX required.

## title
- Purpose: establish the sketch → story → visual workflow.
- Visual: a large headline, short subtitle, and one takeaway.
- Beat 1: reveal the title and context together; pause for the presenter.

## workflow
- Purpose: demonstrate reusable panels, connectors, and deliberate reveals.
- Visual: three equal cards in a horizontal row: Sketch, Story, Visual.
- Beat 1: show Sketch, the rough layout and labels.
- Beat 2: connect to Story, where we choose meaning and reveal order.
- Beat 3: connect to Visual, the editable Manim implementation, and show the takeaway.
- Speaker notes: objects should appear when they become relevant to the explanation.

## Review contract
- Every label fits its card and every arrow connects card edges.
- Use theme colors consistently; no tiny text or unexplained color coding.
- Inspect every pause state, not just the final video frame.
- This is a workflow example, not an automatic Excalidraw converter.
